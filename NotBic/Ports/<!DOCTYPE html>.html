Your confidentiality under CIPSEA

[Placeholder: brief explanation that interviews are conducted under the Confidential Information Protection and Statistical Efficiency Act of 2018 (CIPSEA).]

  • Your responses are used for statistical purposes only.
  • Information is not used for enforcement, regulatory action, or legal proceedings.
  • No identifiable information about individuals or companies is disclosed.
  • Published results include only summary statistics and de-identified examples.
